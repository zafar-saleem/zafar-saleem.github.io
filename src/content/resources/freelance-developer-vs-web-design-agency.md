# Freelance Landing Page Developer vs Web Design Agency: Which Should You Hire?

If your business needs a professional landing page, you have several options. You can hire an independent freelancer, work with a web design agency or use a website builder.

Each option has different advantages depending on your budget, requirements and available resources.

For startup founders and small business owners, choosing between a freelance landing page developer and an agency can be particularly important.

Here's what to consider before making a decision.

## What does a freelance landing page developer do?

A freelance landing page developer works independently with clients to build and launch landing pages.

Depending on their skills and service offering, they may handle:
- Frontend development.
- Figma-to-code implementation.
- Responsive layouts.
- Contact forms and integrations.
- Performance optimisation.
- SEO-friendly implementation.
- Testing and deployment.

One advantage is direct communication with the person responsible for developing your page.

However, freelancers differ in experience and specialisation. Review their portfolio, technical skills, process and availability before hiring.

## What does a web design agency offer?

A web design agency usually operates with a team of specialists.

Depending on the agency, this may include:
- UX and UI designers.
- Brand designers.
- Copywriters.
- Developers.
- Project managers.
- SEO specialists.

This can be useful when your project requires several disciplines, extensive research or a larger team.

The trade-off is that you may communicate with multiple people throughout the project, and the agency's process may involve additional coordination.

## Freelancer vs agency: key differences

### Communication

A freelancer often provides direct communication with the person developing your landing page.

An agency may assign a project manager who coordinates communication between you and the team.

Consider which approach better suits your working style.

### Project scope

A freelancer may be a good fit for a clearly defined landing page development project.

An agency may be more suitable when you need a wider package involving branding, research, copywriting, design and multiple development services.

### Cost

Pricing varies significantly across both freelancers and agencies.

An agency may include a broader range of services and operational costs. A freelancer may provide a more focused scope.

Don't compare quotes based on price alone. Check what is included, who is doing the work and what happens after launch.

### Flexibility

Working directly with a freelancer may allow more direct discussions about implementation decisions and project changes.

Agencies may have established processes that help manage larger projects but can require more formal coordination.

### Accountability

With a freelancer, you generally work directly with the person responsible for delivery.

With an agency, responsibility may be shared across designers, developers and project managers.

In either case, confirm who is accountable for deadlines, revisions, testing and handover.

## When should you hire a freelance landing page developer?

A freelancer may be the right choice if:

- You already have a Figma design.
- You need one focused landing page.
- Your project requirements are clearly defined.
- You want direct communication with your developer.
- You need responsive frontend implementation.
- You want to avoid coordinating a large team.

## When should you hire a web design agency?

An agency may be a better fit if:

- You need complete branding and visual identity work.
- Your project requires extensive user research.
- You need copywriting, design and development together.
- You have a complex project requiring multiple specialists.
- You need ongoing support from a larger team.

## How to choose the right landing page developer

Before hiring someone, review:

1. **Relevant projects:** Have they built landing pages similar to what you need?
2. **Technical experience:** Can they implement responsive layouts and required integrations?
3. **Communication:** Do they explain technical decisions clearly?
4. **Project scope:** Are deliverables, revisions and timelines documented?
5. **Performance:** Do they consider loading speed and usability?
6. **Handover:** Will you receive the files and information you need after launch?

Ask questions and request a clear proposal before committing.
