Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), I process personal information on the following legal bases, where applicable:

Pre-contractual steps

When you contact me to request information, discuss a project, obtain a quotation or explore a potential working relationship.

Contractual necessity

When processing information is necessary to provide services or fulfil an agreement with you.

Legitimate interests

When processing is necessary for reasonable business purposes, such as responding to business communications, maintaining security, preventing spam and managing professional relationships, provided those interests do not override your rights.

Legal obligations

When I need to retain or process information to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

I retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Unsuccessful project enquiries

Active project information

For the duration of the working relationship and any necessary follow-up period.

Invoices and accounting records

For the period required by applicable Portuguese tax and accounting laws.

Legal or dispute-related records

For as long as necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
